### Summary
As reported in #3484, the FA2 `BatchPrefill` kernels (paged + ragged) are  slower with an FP8 KV cache than with BF16. In the FP8 K/V case, the cache was being dequantized to BF16 in registers on every tile and then fed to the same BF16 MMA. That's pure ALU/shuffle

## Problem

perf(attention): Speed up FP8 KV-cache prefill (FA2 BatchPrefill) by repacking K/V to BF16 in shared memory
