## Purpose

On SM 12.x (RTX 50-series, NVIDIA GB10 / DGX Spark), the Marlin and Marlin-MoE kernels are currently *missing* from the compiled `_C.so` / `_moe_C.abi3.so`. The driver tries to JIT-promote the `8.0+PTX` fallback to SM 12.x, and Marlin-MoE silently produces wrong outputs (V4-Flash MoE dec

## Problem
