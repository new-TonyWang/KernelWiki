Notes:

- The previous AIV/UB results were not Cube MTE2 results.
- Direct `GM -> L0A/L0B` modes are present in the source as experimental modes, but host-timed runs produced impossible values (tens to hundreds of TB/s). Those numbers are not reported as valid bandwidth. The reliable Cube data path exposed by the AscendC matmul examples is `GM -> A1/B1` followed by `A1/B1 -> A2/B2`.
